90-day trial

The trial begins September 3, 2026 and runs through December 1, 2026.

Both dates are stated here so the window is visible to anyone reading this page rather than counted from a signup date nobody can see. Human Rights Initiative of North Texas holds Almanac at Standard and Scribe at Pro for this trial, with Infrastructure and Updates included for its full length, covering updates and improvements to the systems behind both products at no additional cost. Nothing on this page shuts itself off on December 1 and no access is cut automatically; what happens after that date is a conversation between Human Rights Initiative of North Texas and MICRO Group, L.L.C., not something the software decides.

Joint funding, two or more organizations

Confluence

Finds funding that connects your organization's mission to one or more named partner organizations' work, including consortiums, associations, and collaboratives, where you remain the eligible applicant. An angle Almanac's single-org matching doesn't surface on its own. Every organization in the group needs its own Almanac subscription; only one needs Scribe, and only one needs Docket.
