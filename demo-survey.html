Tell Us About Your Organization

Almanac, Scribe, and Concordance already pull what's public: a filed Form 990, board rosters, prior grants reported on Schedule I, giving history. This form is for everything that isn't public: your own goals, the problems you're trying to solve, and the documents and history only your organization can supply.

What we already have. Revenue, expenses, net assets, officer compensation, filed NTEE code, and prior reported grants come from your public IRS filings automatically. You don't need to re-enter any of that here.

What we're asking for instead. Current goals and priorities, funder relationships and history beyond what's on file, your own board roster (needed to find who could make an introduction), and copies of documents like your most recent Form 990 and recent grant applications, including ones that weren't funded.

Every question below is optional except your name, organization, and email. Answer what applies and leave the rest blank; a partial answer is still useful.

The Form

Submitting this sends an email directly to Jonathan Lin Davis. It is not a contract, an application, or a request for services, and nothing here is shared beyond that.

PDF, DOC, or DOCX, up to 25 MB total. If a file's easier to share as a link instead, use the field below.
