Docket

Docket carries Organization Project Requests — Letters of Inquiry and Requests for Proposals — separately from Almanac's traditional grant matches. Same sourcing standard: every row traces to a real, currently-checked source.

Every request listed here was verified against a real source (the funder's or agency's own current page): nothing here is estimated or invented. Where something is uncertain, it's flagged explicitly rather than guessed at.
This is a 30-day pilot. Access to this page is scoped to Measure for the pilot window; Organization Project Requests captured here are also being researched for the joint Measure/Texas Empowerment Academy funding project.
