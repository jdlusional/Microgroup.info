Privacy

What this site collects, and why

This page covers microgroup.info: MICRO Group, L.L.C.'s consulting and Enterprise Suite site. It does not cover jonathanlindavis.com, which has its own privacy page for open-data tools. See also the security page for how what you submit is hosted and protected.

Effective 2026-08-21.

Who runs this site

microgroup.info is run by MICRO Group, L.L.C., a research and consulting firm. Where this policy says "we," it means MICRO Group, L.L.C.

What we collect from visitors

Most of this site is descriptive and does not ask for anything. A few forms collect information directly, and they do not all handle it the same way:

  • Contact form. Name, email, phone, organization, and your message. This is stored in our database and a copy is emailed to us.
  • Organization intake survey and Specialist Panel questionnaire. These are email-only: nothing you enter is stored in a database. Your answers, and any document you upload (for example, a Form 990, audited financials, a grant application, a CV, or a biography), are forwarded as a single email to our inbox and are not retained anywhere else by this site.
  • Feature-specific requests (for example a grant-drafter request or a pricing request). Whatever you enter on that specific form, generally your name, email, organization, and the details of the request.

We also use Google Analytics site-wide to understand traffic in aggregate. It is not tied to the forms above.

We do not sell your submission, in whole or in part, to any third party. We do not run ads on this site.

The Specialist Panel questionnaire's publication consent

The Specialist Panel intake form asks nine separate yes/no questions about what may be published about you (for example, your name, your biography, or your photograph). Each one defaults to no: if a question is left blank, or answered with anything other than an explicit yes, it is recorded as no. Declining to answer is treated the same as declining outright, never as permission.

What we collect from Enterprise Suite clients

Client organizations using MICRO Group's Enterprise Suite (Almanac, Scribe, Docket, and related tools) provide real organizational information as part of onboarding: identifying details about the organization, its funders and grant history, and similar program information needed to build that organization's own pages. That information is used to build and operate the client's own Enterprise Suite pages, which are restricted to that organization's own authorized users (see the security page for how). It is not shared with other clients and is not used to build any other organization's pages.

Who else sees it

We use Resend, a transactional email provider, to deliver a copy of form submissions and questionnaire responses to our own inbox. Resend processes that mail on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. We do not otherwise share, license, or sell what you submit to any third party.

How long we keep it

Contact-form submissions are kept until you ask us to delete them; there is no automatic expiration. Survey and Specialist Panel submissions live only in the email they were forwarded to, not in a separate database, since those forms store nothing themselves. Enterprise Suite client data is kept for the duration of the engagement and as otherwise agreed with that client.

Your choices

You can ask us, at any time, to tell you what we have on file for you or your organization, correct it, or delete it. Use the contact form. We will confirm what we did within a reasonable time.

Changes to this page

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above.
