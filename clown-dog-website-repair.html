Repair & Service Free Estimate

Bike repair, done right, by people who ride

Our mechanics have over 50 years of combined experience turning wrenches, and we treat every bike that comes through the door as if it were our own. Shifting dialed in, a broken spoke, a full overhaul — we've been the shop next to UT campus for almost 20 years, and there isn't much we haven't seen.

Every repair starts with a free diagnosis. No work begins until you approve the estimate.

Bring us your bike

Text, call, or email us to set up service, or just drop the bike off at the shop during business hours. We'll take note of anything you're concerned about and give it a thorough head-to-toe look, usually within 1-2 days. Once we've diagnosed it, we'll reach out with our recommendations — and once you approve the work, most repairs turn around in 3-5 days.

Tune-Up Package Options

For the most comprehensive service and the best value, we recommend one of our complete tune-up packages. We also offer à la carte repairs below if you just need a minor fix or adjustment. Learn more about our packages and stop by for your free estimate.

Deluxe Tune Up

$120

Our full tune-up, with a lot more love put into it.

  • Precision wheel truing and hub adjustments
  • Brake adjustments and lubrication
  • Shifter and derailleur adjustments and lubrication
  • Adjust major bearings (headset, bottom bracket)
  • Lube cables, housing, and drivetrain
  • Tighten all components and accessories to torque specs
  • Bike wash and clean (extra-dirty bikes may add labor)
  • Inflate tires to recommended pressure
  • Half off labor for most parts installation

Basic Tune Up

$90

Great for cheaper bikes and bikes in fairly decent shape that just need to be tightened up and dialed in. Less wheel work and cleaning involved.

  • Shifter and derailleur adjustment
  • Lube cables, housing, and drivetrain
  • Tighten all components and accessories, safety check
  • Spot wheel truing with wheels on the bike
  • Frame wipe-down
  • Inflate tires to recommended pressure

Single Speed Tune Up

$80

For BMX bikes, cruisers, fixies, and all single speeds.

  • Precision wheel truing and hub adjustments
  • Brake adjustments and lubrication
  • Adjust major bearings (headset, bottom bracket)
  • Lube cables, housing, and drivetrain
  • Tighten all components and accessories
  • Bike wash and clean
  • Inflate tires to recommended pressure
