Docket

Docket carries Organization Project Requests — Letters of Inquiry and Requests for Proposals — separately from Almanac's traditional grant matches. This page shows what it produces for Riverside Neighbors Collaborative, a fictional Austin community organization, using real, currently-open funding requests.

Riverside Neighbors Collaborative does not exist. The funders and requests below do: every row traces to a real, currently-checked source (the funder's own current page), the same standard Docket applies for a real client. Nothing here is estimated or invented.
