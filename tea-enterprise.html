Almanac

Almanac is a funding-prospect tool combining real IRS grant-history data with a live-researched database of currently-open funding opportunities.

Every funder and grant listed here was verified against a real source (IRS Form 990 filings or the funder's own current website): nothing here is estimated or invented. Where something is uncertain, it's flagged explicitly rather than guessed at.

Checked and Ruled Out: Cycle 1

8 of Texas Empowerment Academy's 55 ranked funder candidates had a currently-open call join by EIN this cycle. All 8 were checked directly against the funder's own page and ruled out; none compiled as a live match. Zero compiled matches this cycle is the honest result of that check, not a data gap.

Joint funding, two or more organizations

Confluence

Finds funding that connects your organization's mission to one or more named partner organizations' work, including consortiums, associations, and collaboratives, where you remain the eligible applicant. An angle Almanac's single-org matching doesn't surface on its own. Every organization in the group needs its own Almanac subscription; only one needs Scribe, and only one needs Docket.
