Who's building this

Clown Dog Bikes has been repairing bikes at 2821 San Jacinto Blvd, just north of the UT Austin campus, since 2000. Repair is the actual business here, not an add-on to a sales floor: a diagnosis first, a free estimate before any work starts, and a turnaround that usually runs a few days after that.

The shop's own description of how it treats a customer has stayed consistent for years: it treats every bike that comes in as if it were its own, and it treats every customer, first flat tire or twentieth overhaul, the same way. Reviewers say much the same thing back, most often about patience with beginners and pricing that lands where it was estimated to land.

Customer reviews across platforms currently average roughly 4.8 to 4.9 out of 5.
