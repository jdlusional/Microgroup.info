What these pages were built from, and what is still missing

Everything on both tools was built from publicly available information only: Black Mamas Village's own website, its public IRS Form 990 and 990-EZ filings, and each funder's own current website. No internal documents were used, requested, or assumed, and no figure was estimated to fill a hole.

That covers most of what a funder match and a first draft need, but not all of it. The following can only come from Black Mamas Village, and each one is currently marked as pending rather than guessed at:

  • Board roster, size, and composition. Several funders score or gate on this directly.
  • The current-year operating budget and program budgets. The public record stops at the most recent filed Form 990.
  • Program-level outcome data: how many people each program serves, and what changes for them.
  • Paid staff count and volunteer count.
  • Prior grant applications and funder reports, so a new draft does not contradict what a funder was already told.
  • Gifts and grants that do not appear in the public filings, including individual, corporate, and in-kind support.
  • Standard application attachments: the IRS determination letter, bylaws, a conflict-of-interest policy, and the fiscal year end date.
  • Any application already in flight, so nothing gets filed twice.

Each tool carries this same list against the specific place the information is needed. See Almanac and Scribe.
