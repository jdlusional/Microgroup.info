Security

How this site is hosted and secured

This page covers microgroup.info: MICRO Group, L.L.C.'s consulting and Enterprise Suite site. It does not cover jonathanlindavis.com, which has its own security page. See also the privacy policy for what is collected and why.

Effective 2026-08-21.

Hosting and transport

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and served over HTTPS. Form submissions, questionnaire responses, and uploaded documents all travel over that same encrypted connection.

Where submitted data lives

The contact form's submissions are stored in Cloudflare D1, a managed database tied to this site's own Cloudflare account, and are not copied to a separate server. The organization intake survey and the Specialist Panel questionnaire work differently: they store nothing on this site at all. Both forward what you submit, including any uploaded document, as a single email to our inbox through Resend, a transactional email provider; Resend does not use that mail for its own purposes. Either way, no submission is copied to a third-party data warehouse or analytics platform beyond the ordinary email delivery described here.

Access to Enterprise Suite client pages

Each Enterprise Suite client organization's own pages (its Almanac, Scribe, Docket, and related tools) sit behind Cloudflare Access, restricted to that organization's own named users by email. One client's pages are not reachable by another client, and an unlisted page is never treated as equivalent to a protected one: where a page needs to be restricted, it is gated at the access-control layer, not merely left off navigation. One exception is stated here plainly rather than left to a general clause: the no-charge pilot page for a single named bank contact is intentionally left ungated. The demo pages (demo.html and its Almanac, Scribe, and other demo tool pages) are sample content, not a real client's data, and are public by design.

Secrets and credentials

API keys and other credentials this site depends on (for example, the key used to send email through Resend) are stored as encrypted environment variables in Cloudflare's own dashboard, not in the site's source code or its public repository.

Abuse and bot mitigation

The contact form uses a standard honeypot field: a field real visitors never see or fill in. A submission that fills it in is quietly discarded rather than stored or emailed. This is a modest, ordinary control, not a claim that every form is immune to abuse.

What we do not claim

MICRO Group, L.L.C. is a small firm, not an enterprise security vendor. Nobody here works security full time, there is no fixed schedule for outside penetration testing, and we hold no certification such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. What is stated above reflects how the site actually works today, and nothing more than that. Client organizations with their own security or compliance requirements should raise them with us directly rather than assume a specific standard is met.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you have found a security issue with this site or an Enterprise Suite client page, please use the contact form and describe what you found. There is no separate security-specific inbox; a report through the contact form reaches us directly.

Changes to this page

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above.
