Live Products

Funder & grant match

Almanac

Finds the funders and open grants that fit an organization, ranks them, and shows the public source behind every one.

Grant drafter

Scribe

Works from an Almanac match to produce a near-submission draft, fact-checked against the organization's own public information, with an explicit list of what only the applicant can supply. Requires an active Almanac subscription.

Live
Open demo →
Organization Project Requests

Docket

Almanac's sibling for Letters of Inquiry and Requests for Proposals, tracked separately from traditional grants. Same sourcing standard: every request traces to the funder's or agency's own current page. Requires an active Almanac subscription.

Live
Open demo →
Joint funding, two or more organizations

Confluence

Finds funding that connects your organization's mission to one or more named partner organizations' work, including consortiums, associations, and collaboratives, where you remain the eligible applicant. An angle Almanac's single-org matching doesn't surface on its own. Every organization in the group needs its own Almanac subscription; only one needs Scribe, and only one needs Docket.

Trustees & introductions

Concordance

Names the people who govern the foundations that already fund this kind of work, shows the grants that prove it, and maps who on a board could make the introduction. It does not estimate anyone's wealth.

In Development
Grant-cycle monitor

Herald

Watches the funder pages behind an Almanac watch-list and reports when a cycle opens, a deadline posts, or guidelines change, showing the actual diff, never an interpretation.

In Development
Corporate foundation benchmark

Atlas

Benchmarks a corporate foundation's giving against real aggregate statistics computed from Texas-registered 990-PF filings, not any single named peer.

In Development
990 reconciliation

Dossier

Checks that a foundation's claimed giving matches its own filed 990-PF numbers, and cross-references a voluntary donation list against public filings.
