Grant-cycle monitor

Herald

Watches the funder pages behind an Almanac watch-list and reports when a cycle opens, a deadline posts, or guidelines change, showing the actual diff, never an interpretation.

Corporate foundation benchmark

Atlas

Benchmarks a corporate foundation's giving against real aggregate statistics computed from Texas-registered 990-PF filings, not any single named peer.

In Development
990 reconciliation

Dossier

Checks that a foundation's claimed giving matches its own filed 990-PF numbers, and cross-references a voluntary donation list against public filings.

In Development

Why these rather than a grant database. A subscription database is curated before the vendor knows a specific organization exists. Someone decides which opportunities belong on the shelf, and every subscriber sees the same shelf. These products reverse that order: a person who has worked in the nonprofit sector reads the organization first, and its shelf is built afterward, against its actual goals and programs rather than a keyword match on its mission statement. That is a claim an organization can test before it pays anything, because every demo above is shown before price is discussed.

Why these rather than a general research tool. The funder records behind these products sit on one EIN spine alongside sources a grants database does not carry: 56,756 federal contract awards, 407,293 federal subawards, 177,378 Federal Audit Clearinghouse single audits across 59 states and territories, and tax years 2015 through 2025 of full IRS Form 990 schedule detail, each count measured 2026-08-31. Joining those to the grant record is what makes a single question answerable in one pass: which funders support this work, are growing their giving, and carry no adverse audit findings.

Each demo is built around a clearly-labelled invented organization, so it can be shown to anyone without exposing a client's research. The funders, foundations, trustees, grants and dollar figures inside them are real, and come from public IRS filings.
