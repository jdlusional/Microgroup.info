How this page is filled in

Nothing appears here without an explicit yes. Each Panel Specialist completes the intake questionnaire, and each publishable field on that form carries its own publication control, set to no by default. A field renders on this page only where that person answered yes to that field. A specialist who has not consented to the display of their own name does not appear at all.

These are Panel Specialists. The Memorandum of Understanding creates no compensation and no volume commitment, so no one listed here is under contract to MICRO Group by virtue of appearing on this page.

To correct anything shown here, to withdraw a single field, or to be removed entirely, write to jonathan@microgroup.info. Removal is done on request, without a reason and without a discussion.
