Research, data, and operations capacity for nonprofits and public institutions

MICRO Group, L.L.C. is a strategic, research-driven consultancy serving nonprofit, government, and nonpartisan policy organizations, through expertise in Management, Integration, Culture, Research, and Operations.

Most small organizations are told they need research and data capacity they cannot afford to staff. The work still has to happen: grants researched, bills tracked, systems integrated, results measured. So it lands on people hired to do something else, and it competes with the mission they were hired for.

Services

Five consulting disciplines. Each one names what is done and what you receive.

Management

Strategic direction and leadership capacity for institutions in transition or growth, including interim and fractional leadership while a permanent hire is recruited.

Integration

Programs, teams, software, and data brought into one working system, including donor and constituent database management, with training for the people who use it.

Culture

Team building, employee engagement, and facilitation for organizations through change, delivered as structured sessions with written follow-through rather than a single workshop.

Research

Applied research grounded in the founder's legislative background: policy and bill analysis, committee and district-level data, and funding landscape review.

Operations

Operational management: process documentation, vendor and contract review, and reporting systems, so the work does not depend on one person's memory.

How the policy practice is scoped

The legislative and policy practice is nonpartisan. It covers bill and committee analysis, district-level data, and administrative research for organizations on any side of an issue. It does not include partisan campaign work, and nonprofit and government engagements are never staffed from it.

Jonathan Lin Davis, founder

Funding landscape review
