Pricing

Standing annual rates for Almanac, the required base product; Scribe and Docket, each an add-on that assumes an active Almanac subscription; purchasable additional research depth and application capacity; Confluence, an annual joint-funding rate for two or more organizations; and an optional plan that keeps every product an organization has purchased on current infrastructure. This is the same rate card every organization sees; nothing here is negotiated in the abstract before a request comes in.

Current prices are guaranteed through August 31, 2026: any request submitted and confirmed by that date locks in the rates shown here, regardless of any later change to this page. The guarantee date is mechanically extended to the end of the current calendar month each time a work session starts; it is never extended silently mid-month.

Unlike the product demo pages, this page describes Jonathan Lin Davis's actual current pricing, not a simulation against a fictional organization. Submitting the form below does not create a contract; it opens a conversation, and final terms are confirmed in writing.

Products & pricing

Each product is priced and purchased on its own. There is no bundle discount: buying more than one product is the sum of what's listed below.

Almanac is the required base product. Scribe and every other product on this page, in development or already for sale, is an add-on that assumes an active Almanac subscription. None of them are sold on their own.

Purchasable increments

Additional capacity

Almanac and Docket's add-on is priced proportionally to what an organization already pays: the increase in cost matches the increase in research depth requested, and any portion that turns out undeliverable is prorated back. Scribe's add-on is priced per application, with no cap and no complexity tiering. The two work differently because the two products are different: Almanac and Docket depend on what funders happen to have open in the world; Scribe produces a document MICRO Group, L.L.C. fully controls producing.

Almanac add-on · proportional

Almanac Additional Research Depth

Priced proportionally: N× the depth costs N× your current annual rate

Example: Almanac Standard at $1,500/yr requesting double depth (2×) pays an additional $1,500/yr, $3,000/yr total. Triple depth (3×) pays an additional $3,000/yr, $4,500/yr total. The same ratio applies from Pro's $3,000/yr base. Each multiple buys a proportionally deeper research pass: more candidate funders checked, broader geography and NTEE net, not the same shelf re-rendered.

If the shelf's actual growth falls short of the multiple purchased, because the funders genuinely available at that depth don't exist yet, not because the research wasn't done, the shortfall is prorated and refunded or credited forward at the organization's choice. No fixed cap on the multiple; scheduled only after founder review, since delivery here scales with a person's research time.
Docket add-on · proportional

Docket Additional Research Depth

Priced proportionally: N× the depth costs N× your current annual rate

The same proportional pricing as Almanac's, against Docket's LOI/RFP shelf instead: broader geography and funder-type net checked, not the same shelf re-rendered. LOI and RFP calls are structurally rarer than traditional grants, so a Depth Pass here is more likely to confirm there isn't more to find than to surface a large new batch, and the proration below is where that reality gets accounted for rather than absorbed silently.

Same proration/refund/credit rule as Almanac's: any shortfall between the multiple purchased and what genuinely exists to find is prorated back. No fixed cap on the multiple; same founder-review scheduling gate.
Scribe add-on · per application

Scribe Additional Application

Standard base: $375.00/app · additional: $562.50/app Pro base: $250.00/app · additional: $375.00/app

Each base tier's per-application rate is its annual price divided by its included application count (Standard: $1,500 ÷ 4 = $375.00/app; Pro: $3,000 ÷ 12 = $250.00/app). Every additional application beyond the included count is priced at that same per-app rate plus 50%. No quarterly or monthly increment to buy into: purchase individual additional applications as needed, and there is no cap on how many an organization can buy.

No length or complexity tiering. A 50-page federal application costs the same as a 10-page one; the price is per completed application, not per page or per hour. If a billing period has no funder with a genuine, enterable open call to draft toward, that application credits forward rather than forcing a draft against a poor match.
Joint funding · two or more organizations

Confluence

Confluence finds funding whose own stated priorities genuinely span every organization in a group, whether that's a single named partner, a small consortium, or a larger association or collaborative.

Each organization's own Almanac cost depends on the product it chooses independently of the others: Standard at $1,500/yr or Pro at $3,000/yr, exactly the rates on the Products & pricing table above. One organization can hold Standard while another holds Pro; nothing about Confluence requires them to match. Once every organization in the group has its own active Almanac subscription, Confluence itself is priced flat, on top of whatever each member already pays for Almanac.

Annual · first pair of organizations

Confluence

First pair (2 orgs): $1,500 / year Each additional org: +$500 / year

A dedicated cross-organization research pass, sorted Pursue, Consider, and Checked & Ruled Out, the same structure already live for a real client. Priced below a fresh Almanac build because it reuses each member organization's already-verified Almanac research rather than researching from nothing.

Requires every named organization in the group to hold its own active Almanac subscription before scheduling, not the lead organization only. This is not a formality: Confluence's cost basis depends on each member's Almanac research already being done and already paid for.

Re-runs the cross-organization check on a standing cadence instead of leaving the group's initial shortlist to go stale. Priced below Almanac's own Standard/Pro rates, since a refresh re-checks an existing bridge between organizations rather than rebuilding a shelf from nothing.

Two organizations, independent Almanac tiers, one drafting seat, one LOI/RFP seat: Org A holds Almanac Pro ($3,000/yr) + Scribe + Docket; Org B holds only Almanac Standard ($1,500/yr).
