Pricing

Standing annual rates for Almanac, the required base product; Scribe and every other product, each an add-on that assumes an active Almanac subscription; purchasable additional research depth and application capacity; and an optional plan that keeps every product an organization has purchased on current infrastructure. This is the same rate card every organization sees; nothing here is negotiated in the abstract before a request comes in.

Current prices are guaranteed through August 31, 2026: any request submitted and confirmed by that date locks in the rates shown here, regardless of any later change to this page. The guarantee date is mechanically extended to the end of the current calendar month each time a work session starts; it is never extended silently mid-month.

Unlike the product demo pages, this page describes Jonathan Lin Davis's actual current pricing, not a simulation against a fictional organization. Submitting the form below does not create a contract; it opens a conversation, and final terms are confirmed in writing.

Products & pricing

Each product is priced and purchased on its own. There is no bundle discount: buying more than one product is the sum of what's listed below.

Almanac is the required base product. Scribe and every other product on this page, in development or already for sale, is an add-on that assumes an active Almanac subscription. None of them are sold on their own.

Additional capacity

Both require an active subscription to the product they extend. Neither promises a fixed count: what each buys is described honestly below, because the underlying research and drafting effort does not scale on a fixed schedule.

Almanac add-on

Additional Research Depth

Quarterly: +$2,000 / year Monthly: +$6,000 / year

One additional dedicated research pass run at each refresh, roughly doubling the candidate funders checked against the organization's profile: broader geography and NTEE net, not the same shelf re-rendered. The result count is disclosed honestly each cycle, not guaranteed; some refreshes may find nothing further worth adding.

Capped at one Depth Pass per organization. Requestable directly; scheduled only after founder review, since delivery here scales with a person's research time, not with the price charged for it. Beyond one Depth Pass, this becomes a custom-scoped engagement, not a further fixed-price tier.
Scribe add-on

Additional Application

Quarterly: +$2,000 / year Monthly: +$6,000 / year

One extra completed, submission-ready draft on top of the base subscription's included cadence, at $500 per application, the same per-draft rate the base tiers are built on. Purchasable as a standing quarterly increment (one extra draft every quarter) or a standing monthly increment (one extra draft every month), and stackable.

Capped at 5 additional applications per month, combined across however the increments were purchased. If a billing period has no funder with a genuine, enterable open call to draft toward, that period's increment credits forward rather than forcing a draft against a poor match.
