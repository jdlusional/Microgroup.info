Pricing

Current prices are guaranteed through September 30, 2026: any request submitted and confirmed by that date locks in the rates shown here, regardless of any later change to this page. The guarantee date is mechanically extended to the end of the current calendar month each time a work session starts; it is never extended silently mid-month.

Unlike the product demo pages, this page describes Jonathan Lin Davis's actual current pricing, not a simulation against a fictional organization. Submitting the form below does not create a contract; it opens a conversation, and final terms are confirmed in writing.

Products & pricing

Funder & grant match · Base product

Almanac

Ranked funder and open-grant matches for one organization, every match sourced to the funder's own public filing or page.

Guaranteed: a shelf of at least 100 genuinely available grant opportunities for every organization that signs, or a full refund.
Standard
$1,500 / year
Matches refreshed quarterly (4 / year)
Pro
$3,000 / year
Matches refreshed monthly (12 / year)
Need deeper research? Add depth

Double the research costs double your rate. Almanac Standard at $1,500/year paying for 2× depth adds $1,500, so $3,000/year in total; 3× adds $3,000, so $4,500/year. The same ratio applies from Pro's $3,000 base.

Each step buys a genuinely wider search: more funders checked, more geography, a broader NTEE net. It is not the same shelf run again.

If we find less than you paid for, because funders at that depth do not exist yet rather than because the work was not done, you get the shortfall back as a refund or a credit forward, your choice. There is no cap on the multiple; deeper passes are scheduled after review, since delivery scales with a person's research time.

Grant drafter

Scribe

A complete, submission-ready draft each cycle, fact-checked against the organization's own public record, with an explicit list of what only the applicant can supply.

Standard
$1,500 / year
1 application drafted per quarter (4 / year)
Pro
$3,000 / year
1 application drafted per month (12 / year), for a larger or more continuous pipeline
Requires an active Almanac subscription
Need more applications than your tier includes?

Buy them one at a time. Standard: $562.50 per extra application. Pro: $375.00 per extra application.

Those come from each tier's own included rate plus 50%: Standard is $1,500 ÷ 4 = $375.00 an application, Pro is $3,000 ÷ 12 = $250.00. There is no cap and no increment to buy into.

No length or complexity pricing. A 50-page federal application costs the same as a 10-page one. And if a period has no genuine open call worth writing toward, that application credits forward instead of forcing a draft against a poor match.

Organization Project Requests

Docket

Almanac's sibling for Letters of Inquiry and Requests for Proposals, tracked separately from traditional grants. Same sourcing standard: every request traces to the funder's or agency's own current page.

Standard
$1,500 / year
Shelf refreshed quarterly (4 / year)
Pro
$3,000 / year
Shelf refreshed monthly (12 / year)
Requires an active Almanac subscription.
Need deeper research? Add depth

The same proportional pricing as Almanac's, against Docket's LOI and RFP shelf: N× the depth costs N× your current annual rate.

LOI and RFP calls are rarer than traditional grants, so a deeper pass here often confirms there is not more to find. If it falls short, you get the difference back or credited forward, rather than paying for research the world did not have. No cap on the multiple; same review before scheduling.

Example: Almanac Standard (required base) + Scribe Pro (add-on) + Infrastructure & Updates = $1,500 + $3,000 + $2,000 = $6,500 / year.
This is a straight sum shown for illustration, not a discounted bundle rate.
Docket follows the same required-base rule as Scribe: Almanac Standard + Docket Standard = $1,500 + $1,500 = $3,000 / year, one organization, no bundle discount.
Joint funding · two or more organizations

Confluence

Funding whose own priorities genuinely span every organization in a group, whether that is one named partner, a small consortium, or a larger collaborative. Every organization needs its own active Almanac subscription first; the tiers do not have to match.

Annual · on top of each member's Almanac

Confluence

First pair (2 orgs): $1,500 / year Each additional org: +$500 / year

A cross-organization research pass sorted Pursue, Consider, and Checked & Ruled Out. Priced below a fresh Almanac build because it reuses each member's verified research rather than starting from nothing.

Optional: keep it current

Confluence Refresh re-runs the check on a standing cadence instead of letting the group's shortlist go stale. Quarterly: +$1,000 / year. Monthly: +$3,000 / year. Requires an active Confluence subscription for the group.

Example: Org A holds Almanac Pro ($3,000/yr) plus Scribe; Org B holds Almanac Standard ($1,500/yr). Org A's Scribe seat drafts the joint application.
Confluence adds $1,500/yr on top, whichever tiers they chose.

Covers regular updates and improvements to the infrastructure behind every product the organization has purchased, whether that's one product or all of them. It is optional, recommended, and billed once per organization rather than once per product.
