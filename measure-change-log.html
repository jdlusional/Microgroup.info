Measure Change Log

Measure's own dated record, grouped by product (Almanac, Scribe, Docket, Confluence). Two kinds of entry, each labeled: Hand change is a one-off edit to Measure's own account (a profile correction, a configuration change); Shelf digest is a one-sentence monthly summary of that month's Almanac or Docket shelf refresh, never itemized per funder. The org-agnostic engineering record for every product, across every organization, is kept separately on jonathanlindavis.com's System Change Log.

Updated continuously
