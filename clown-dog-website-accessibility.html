Accessibility

Our commitment to making this site usable for everyone.

This wording is deliberately a general commitment rather than a specific technical standard claim, since the site's accessibility work (alt text, keyboard navigation, screen-reader testing) is still in progress on this preview build.

Clown Dog Bikes is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including customers who use assistive technology. We're actively working on accessibility as we build out this new site — things like clear image descriptions, keyboard navigation, and readable contrast — and we'll keep improving it after launch, not just at launch.

If you run into anything on this site that's hard to use, please contact us and let us know. We'll do our best to help right away and to fix the underlying issue.
